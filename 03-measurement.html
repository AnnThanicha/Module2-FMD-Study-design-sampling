3. Measurement: what will we record?

An operational definition states exactly how a variable is measured, precisely enough that two investigators record the same value for the same animal.

3.1 Six variable types

TypeExampleSummaryTypical test / model
ContinuousMilk yield, kg/dayMean & SD (or median & IQR)t-test, ANOVA, linear regression
BinaryInfection yes/noProportionChi-square, logistic regression
NominalBreedNumber & % per categoryChi-square
OrdinalBody condition scoreOrdered distribution, medianRank tests, ordinal regression
CountDiarrhoea episodesCount distribution, ratePoisson / negative binomial
Time-to-eventDays until first episodeSurvival probabilitiesKaplan–Meier, Cox

Classify the variable

Example case definition“A calf with faecal score ≥3 on a specified four-point scale, at any scheduled assessment during its first 30 days of life.” Also state the scoring instructions, who scores, and how often: daily scoring finds more short episodes than weekly scoring.
Avoid information lossRecord variables in their richest form. You can turn milk yield into “high/low” later, but you cannot recover kg/day from a yes/no field. Dichotomising also reduces power.
