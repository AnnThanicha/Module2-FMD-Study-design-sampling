Step 1

1. The study objective

A study objective is the single question the study is built to answer. It drives every later choice: who you sample, what you measure, how many animals you need and which analysis you run.

Build an objective

Start from the weak objective “To study mastitis.” Switch on each element and watch the objective become workable.

1.1 Estimate, compare or detect?

PurposeQuestionExampleDrives sample size
EstimateHow much or how common?Proportion of lactating cows with mastitisPrecision and confidence
Compare / associationDo groups differ?Is calf diarrhoea more frequent with inadequate colostrum?Minimum important difference, α and power
Detect infectionWould we detect infection if present at a set level?Cattle to test to support herd freedomDesign prevalence, test sensitivity, confidence
TipsUse an action verb that matches the purpose. Keep one primary objective (sample size is calculated for it). Write it so a colleague could design the same study from it.
