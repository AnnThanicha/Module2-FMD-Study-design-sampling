Objectives, measurement, sampling design, sample-size calculation, and interpreting the result. Every example here is interactive: change the numbers and watch the answer move.

Eight steps, in order

Every study in this module is planned with the same eight steps. If you change the objective, you must revisit everything below it.

StepQuestion it answersSection
1. ObjectiveWhat question are we asking?1
2. VariablesWhich is the outcome, which the exposure, and what else could explain the link?2
3. MeasurementWhat will we record, and how?3
4. Target population and sampling unitWho do we want to draw conclusions about, and what exactly are we selecting?4
5. Study designHow will we answer the question?5
6. Sampling methodHow do we pick the units?6
7. Sample sizeHow many units do we need?7
8. EvaluationWhat do the data show? (OR, RR, chi-square)8

Learning outcomes

  1. Objectives & measurement – write a clear objective and define the outcome and how it is measured.
  2. Variables – identify dependent and independent variables, and recognise confounders.
  3. Sampling unit & study design – identify the sampling unit and choose a suitable design.
  4. Sampling method – select a method that suits the target population.
  5. Sample size – estimate or compare means, prevalence and proportions; plan case-control and cohort studies.
  6. Clustering & adjustments – explain how clustering, finite populations and missing data change the plan.
  7. Evaluation – calculate and interpret OR, RR, chi-square and Mantel–Haenszel results.
